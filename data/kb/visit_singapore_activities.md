# Visit Singapore — Activities
Source: Visit Singapore
Source URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/

Singapore's attractions span city viewpoints, heritage districts, gardens, food-focused experiences and family-friendly activities. Common planning areas include Marina Bay, Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Gelam and Sentosa.

For a first visit, combining major city sights with one heritage area and one slower-paced garden or waterfront stop gives a balanced itinerary.

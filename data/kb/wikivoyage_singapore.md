# Wikivoyage — Singapore
Source: Wikivoyage
Source URL: https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Singapore

Singapore is a compact city-state with distinct neighbourhoods and a strong mix of modern architecture, cultural districts and food destinations. Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam and the Marina Bay area are useful anchors for a city itinerary.

For neighbourhood exploration, grouping nearby stops helps avoid unnecessary travel time.
